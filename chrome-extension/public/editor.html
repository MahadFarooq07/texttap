Tips for a better capture

Crop closely around the text. Use a clear image with letters at least 20 pixels tall. Choose “Single text block” for a paragraph, “Single line” for a heading, or “Scattered text” for disconnected labels. Rotation is manual. For a complex table, select Table / TSV and review the columns before copying. Handwriting, curved text, and damaged scans are not reliably supported.

Formatting is rule-based and cannot reconstruct every original font, emphasis, or merged table cell. It never uses a language model to guess or rewrite your content.
